PillNote

Medical professionals have long struggled with the problem of medication adherence. Diseases such as hypertension, diabetes mellitus, chronic colitis, as well as many other chronic conditions are significantly affected by medication non-adherence and can result in adverse patient outcomes. Many clinical studies support the necessity of interventions to improve medication adherence.

TeraFlip has developed a product that effectively addresses the issue, PillNote, and has the potential to improve patient outcomes in two ways. First, by providing the physician and caregiver a closer look at patient compliance and secondly making a medication regimen easier to remember and follow. PillNote is a simple and comprehensive application for iOS devices, soon Android and a web interface, that enhances a user’s interaction with their medications.

PillNote helps resolve the unmet need of an easy-to-use application that increases patient adherence with chronic medication protocols. The obvious result of our unique system includes improved patient outcomes resulting in more efficient and effective patient care.

Project Details:

  • Medication Entry
  • Medication Reminders
  • Refill Reminders
  • Offline Database
  • Image Database
  • Drug Search
  • Pill Info
  • Reports
  • Email Updates
