About Me

  • From typing my name on Windows 3.1 at the age of 3, to building my own computer at the age of 11, to developing websites and iOS apps in my freetime; I fell in love with computers from a young age and have pursued my passion ever since.

    I am a PhD Computer Science student at UMBC, having completed both my Bachelor's and Master's in 4.5 years in Fall 2014. In my Master's work I explored visual analytics and machine learning in user interfaces to develop an approach for learning and generating suggestions from user interactions. For my PhD, I am using visual analytics and machine learning to learn from unstructured and sparse temporal data with the intent of being able to predict future events and sequences in the data.

    I also work as a Visual Analytic Scientist/Developer at the National Intrepid Center of Excellence (NICoE) at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center in Bethesda, MD where I research healthcare data and apply machine learning and visualization techniques to learn patterns and trends from the data with the goal of preventive healthcare.

    As my lifelong goal, I always look to further my knowledge and education. There is no boundary to what I can learn and accomplish.
